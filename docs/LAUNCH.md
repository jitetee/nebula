**Nebula 0.9.7: one look, four apps, one community store**

We just shipped Nebula 0.9.7 from J.I.T.E.T.E.E.: a free terminal toolkit for Termux made by tech-minded people.

- **One look everywhere.** Nebula, 19 games, Mingle (18+) and ant now share the same green, framed style as jtlearn: banners, menus, tables, chat bubbles.
- **JIF Store.** Share and download `.jif` (JITETEE Import File) lessons on the website, rate them, or submit your own. jtlearn reads the same store.
- **ant**: an AI coding agent for your terminal, pure Python, no Node.
- **jtlearn**: notes, quizzes, courses and Rage Bait, all offline-friendly.
- **Termux ENTER key** on the extra-keys row.

Install: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jitetee/nebula/main/install/install.sh | bash`
Source: github.com/jitetee/nebula · Website: https://jitetee-ke.web.app

Found a bug or have an idea? Run `nebula report`.
